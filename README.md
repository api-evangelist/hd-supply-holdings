# HD Supply Holdings (hd-supply-holdings)

HD Supply is one of the largest industrial distributors in North America,
providing maintenance, repair, and operations products and value-added
services to multifamily, hospitality, healthcare, and government facilities.
HD Supply operates as a wholly owned subsidiary of The Home Depot following
its acquisition in 2020.
